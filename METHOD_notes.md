## THE 3-STEP CORE: "ICC" (Indication → Correction → Continuation)
ICT-derived pure price-action method. NO INDICATORS. The whole strategy = wait for I, wait for C, enter on C.

### STEP 1 — INDICATION  (timeframe: 1H / 4H, sometimes daily)
- Indication = price BREAKS a swing HIGH (bullish) or swing LOW (bearish).
- It is the START of a trend. It answers "what is the trend? where is price headed?"
- Rules:
  - Indications can ONLY come from a swing high/low being broken.
  - Swing highs/lows = pivots where price rejects and reverses (same as HH/HL/LH/LL).
  - DO NOT TRADE the indication/breakout directly. Treat it as INFORMATION/blueprint: it tells you direction + where your TP will be.
  - Levels COST MONEY to break → hold strong on HTF. Market accumulates liquidity in consolidation then bursts out (like shaking a Coke bottle).
  - Don't chase → causes FOMO/greed. Set alerts; just NOTE new highs/lows.
- Prefer 1H; use 4H when 1H is choppy/unclear. 4H = long-term swings; 1H = day-trade swings (often turned into swing holds).

### STEP 2 — CORRECTION  (timeframe: MONITOR ON 15-MINUTE)
- Correction = the pullback AFTER a new high/low that GRABS LIQUIDITY and liquidates FOMO breakout traders.
- It's psychology/money-flow: breakout draws in trapped buyers (advanced at breakout + beginners chasing the top with heavy positions); once buyers are exhausted vs sellers, market reverses to take them out.
- Don't trade breakouts because of this liquidity grab — could be strong, subtle, or none.
- CORRECTION IS WATCHED ON THE 15M (on 1H it's just 1–2 candles down).
- Knowing when the correction is OVER → that's your entry trigger (Day 4+).

### STEP 3 — CONTINUATION  (entry on 15M)
- After correction, price resumes back above the swing high (buy) / below the swing low (sell).
- ENTRY here. SL = below/above the previous structure level (the correction's extreme). TP = the indication's high/low; if trend continues, new highs/lows → hold & maximize.
- If a real trend, price will NOT break its structure → can hold swing for 3-4+ days.
- Scale into position; scale out (take partials) at a level but keep the runner. Day trade → swing.
- Buying = buying into sellers; Selling = selling into buyers (counterparty swap).

## MARKET STRUCTURE FUNDAMENTALS (Day 1)
- 3 conditions: UPTREND, DOWNTREND, CONSOLIDATION.
- Uptrend = Higher Highs + Higher Lows. Downtrend = Lower Highs + Lower Lows.
- DO NOT TRADE consolidation (no swings being broken = "breaking the rules"). Wait.
- Price won't break HTF structure unless reversing or consolidating.
- Everything follows rules; need a plan.
