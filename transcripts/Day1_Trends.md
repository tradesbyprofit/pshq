== KEY METHOD (extracted) ==
- Trading = buy or sell at a price. Buy profits when price goes ABOVE entry; sell profits when price goes BELOW entry. NOT gambling — you have a strategic, logical reason.
- Tools needed: a forex broker, TradingView (charting), Wi-Fi, and a crypto brokerage (Coinbase/Kraken) used to deposit/withdraw funds via Bitcoin.
- THE 3 MARKET CONDITIONS you must identify: UPTREND, DOWNTREND, CONSOLIDATION.
  - DOWNTREND = lower highs (LH) + lower lows (LL).
  - UPTREND = higher highs (HH) + higher lows (HL).
  - Highs/Lows = pivot points where price reaches an extreme then shifts direction.
- Trade the HIGHER TIME FRAME (he uses the 1-hour, sometimes 4-hour) — it is stronger and more reliable than small timeframes.
- Price will NOT break trend structure on the higher TF UNLESS it is (a) intending to reverse, or (b) consolidating.
- CONSOLIDATION = price breaking highs/lows back and forth, sideways, "no rules." DO NOT TRADE IT. Avoid markets breaking rules.
- Everything in trading follows rules (fundamentals, technicals). You need a plan.
- Tease: ICT ("ICC") — a 3-step process for entering, exiting, and using a stop-loss, taught later in the course.
- He primarily trades NASDAQ (also covers XAUUSD/Gold and BTC in the course).
