## 1. THE CORE: "ICC" = Indication → Correction → Continuation

The entire method is a deliberately minimal 3-step loop. Fewer steps = fewer mistakes. ICC is "kindergarten-level price action" — nothing mystical, not trading like the banks, **not** "break & retest," **not** supply/demand, **not** indicators or chart patterns.

The desired behavior of any healthy trend is for this loop to **repeat**: Indication → Correction → Continuation → (new) Indication → …

### STEP 1 — INDICATION  (markup: 1H / 4H, sometimes Daily)
- **Definition:** Price **breaks a swing HIGH** (→ bullish indication) or **breaks a swing LOW** (→ bearish indication).
- **Absolute rule:** *An indication ALWAYS creates a new high or a new low. If it makes neither, it is NOT an indication — do not act.*
- **Purpose:** It is the **starting point of a trend**. It answers *"what is the trend?"* and *"where is price headed?"*
- **Do NOT trade the indication/breakout.** Treat it purely as **information** — a blueprint that tells you direction + where your TP will later sit.
- **Why levels hold:** Levels "cost money" to break, so HTF levels are strong. The market accumulates liquidity in consolidation, then bursts out "like a shaken Coke bottle." Don't chase the burst — it's FOMO/greed. **Set alerts and note the new high/low.**
- **Timeframe:** Prefer **1H**; drop to **4H** only when the 1H is choppy/unclear. (4H = long-term swing bias; 1H = day-trade bias.)

### STEP 2 — CORRECTION  (monitor on 15M; 5M if cleaner)
- **Definition:** The pullback that happens **after** a new high/low — its job is to **grab liquidity** and liquidate the FOMO/breakout traders before the real move.
- **Psychology behind it:** The breakout pulls in trapped traders — advanced traders near the breakout + beginners chasing the top with heavy positions. Once buyers (in a bullish breakout) are exhausted vs sellers, price reverses to take them out.
- **Why you wait:** Don't trade breakouts *because* of this liquidity grab — it can be strong, subtle, or absent. The correction is what **filters fakeouts**.
- **Watch it on the 15M** (on the 1H it's just 1–2 candles down; the 15M shows the internal structure). Use 5M if it's clearer.
- **It doesn't have to retrace all the way** to the prior level — a *shallow* correction is fine and actually signals the dominant side is stronger.
- Knowing **when the correction is over** is the entry trigger (Step 3).

### STEP 3 — CONTINUATION  (entry on 15M/5M)
- **Definition:** After the correction, price **resumes back through** the broken swing level a **SECOND time** — confirming the trend and that the liquidity grab is done.
- **The single most important discipline:** *Never enter on the first break (indication).* **Always wait for the second time around.** That second confirmation is what saves you from fakeouts.
- **Entry mechanics (sell example — mirror for buys):**
  1. 1H breaks a swing low → **Indication** (new low).
  2. Price bounces → **Correction**. On the 15M/5M, track the internal highs/lows (HH/HL building during the bounce).
  3. Watch for the internal structure to **fail**: e.g. equal highs → a **lower high** → then it **breaks the higher-low support** → and breaks back **below the 1H level**.
  4. That confluence = **enter the sell**.
- **Stop-loss:** Just beyond the prior structure (above the lower high / below the higher low of the correction). Give it room (esp. on volatile instruments). Place it at the **reaction level**, not a random spot.
- **Take-profit:** The **origin of the indication** — the new low/high the indication created. **If price surpasses TP, you've caught a runner** → hold and maximize (the trend keeps making new extremes).
- **For a safer entry**, enter from just beyond the **1H** level (more momentum there) rather than the 5M micro-level.
- You generally **don't need to wait for the candle close** once all confirmations are aligned — but for the bot we will require a body close for safety (see Day 12).

## 2. MARKET STRUCTURE — the only "indicator"

No RSI, no MACD, no chart patterns. **Swing highs and swing lows are your only clues.**

| Structure | Sequence | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Uptrend** | Higher High → Higher Low → HH → HL … | Buyers in control; bullish |
| **Downtrend** | Lower High → Lower Low → LH → LL … | Sellers in control; bearish |
| **Consolidation / Range** | Swings broken back & forth, no clean HH/HL or LH/LL | "No rules" — **DO NOT TRADE** |

### The mandatory-sequence rule
- In an uptrend: **a HH must be followed by a HL; a HL must be followed by a HH.**
- In a downtrend: **a LH must be followed by a LL.**
- Break of this sequence = **structure break = potential reversal** (your exit / reassess signal).

### The "respect" principle (Day 14)
- **Higher timeframe = highly respected. Lower timeframe = loses respect.**
- HTF = **1H, 4H, Daily.** LTF = **15M** (he doesn't use 30M).
- **Mark up ONLY on HTF** (find the most-respected previous high & low). Then the LTF entry must **correspond/correlate** with that HTF markup (the LTF is the "level-1 player" that must average up to the "level-10" HTF level).
- Advanced traders can trade off HTF alone — the LTF just adds precision.

### The bias rules (the "position in structure" lens)
- **Anything ABOVE a swing low (HTF) = potential BULLISH.**
- **Anything BELOW a swing high (HTF) = potential BEARISH.**
- **THE ENTRY GATE (Day 12, explicit rules):**
  - **To BUY → price must break ABOVE a swing high.**
  - **To SELL → price must break BELOW a swing low.**
  - Until that level breaks, you do **not** have permission to trade that direction. If it has a high and a low but **no break = NO TRADE ZONE**.

### Marking the structure
- Mark **body closes**, not wicks. *"Body closes are closed deals; wicks are 'we were here but didn't succeed.'"* (Wicks can be marked on the LTF for finer entries, but structural swings = bodies.)
- **Look back only 1–3 sessions** (not literal days — trading sessions). Gold lives in **London + New York** sessions. You'll find your swings within ~3 sessions. Don't clutter the chart; one to a few clean levels only.

## 3. TIMEFRAME CORRELATION (Day 6) — everything must line up

- **Everything under the 1H must correlate with the 1H.** You only scale down **during the Correction → Continuation** — that's the only time you need the LTF.
- Workflow each session:
  1. Open **1H**, mark levels (where are buyers? where are sellers?).
  2. Wait for an **indication** (a level breaks).
  3. Wait for the **correction**, then scale to **15M/5M** and look for **the same ICC pattern to repeat on the LTF**.
  4. Enter when the LTF confirms (lower highs + breaking supports + below the 1H level, for a sell).
- **Bigger TF overpowers smaller:** 4H > 1H > 30M > 15M. If TFs **contradict**, be patient and wait for extra confirmation; the 1H can "build into" the 4H. Go with whichever TF shows the **cleanest structure**.
- **HTF gives you the LEVELS; the LTF gives you PRECISION** ("the finishing cut" of the trade).

## 5. REVERSALS & EXITS (Day 9, 11)

- **Price always maintains structure.** A trend holds its sequence until it breaks.
- **Reversal signal:** The moment price **breaks the defining swing of the trend** —
  - In a downtrend, a break of a **lower high** (or appearance of a **higher low**) = the downtrend is in doubt → stop assuming; reassess.
  - In an uptrend, a break of a **higher low** (or a **lower high**) = the uptrend is in doubt.
- **Exit logic:** If price starts printing structure **against your trade** (e.g. you're short and it makes a higher low / breaks a lower high), **exit**. Trail or hold the runner only while structure holds in your favor.
- **Don't flip bias daily.** *"Since I sold Monday I should look for buys Tuesday"* — wrong. **Stick with the trend** that's working; don't be "creative." Follow what price is giving you.
- **Trends don't pause** — a real trend keeps going (may consolidate to build capital, then resumes). Find trending markets and ride them.
- **Never feel you "missed it."** Wait for the secure, higher-probability second-time entry rather than catching the exact top/bottom.

## 7. THE EXACT TRADE CHECKLIST (codified for the bot)

A setup is only valid if **all** of these are true:

**A. Context / Markup (HTF 1H or 4H)**
1. Market is in a clean **trend** (HH/HL or LH/LL), **not consolidation**. Look back ≤3 sessions.
2. A swing high/low is **broken** → an **Indication** (new extreme made).
3. Bias confirmed by the entry gate: want **buy** → above a broken swing high; want **sell** → below a broken swing low.

**B. Setup formation (wait — don't chase)**
4. Price **corrects** back toward/below the level (liquidity grab). No trade yet.
5. You are in a **trading session** (London/NY for gold; any for BTC).

**C. Entry trigger (LTF 15M or 5M)**
6. The **second-time continuation**: internal LTF structure flips against the correction (e.g. for a sell: LH printed, then the HL support breaks, then price reclaims the 1H level to the downside).
7. A **body close** confirms beyond the level.
8. All timeframes **correlate** (HTF and LTF agree on direction).

**D. Trade parameters**
9. **Entry:** just beyond the HTF level on the continuation.
10. **Stop-loss:** just past the correction's extreme structure (give room).
11. **Take-profit:** the indication's origin (prior new extreme). Hold a runner while structure holds.
12. **Risk:** fixed fractional risk per trade (e.g. 0.5–1% of account). **No trade if R:R is poor.**

**If ANY step is missing → NO TRADE.** This is exactly the "1 trade a week, sometimes zero, never chase" philosophy you asked for.
